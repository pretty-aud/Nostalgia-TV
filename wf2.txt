THE CHILD'S SIZE IS MAINTAINED BY ONE LOSSY CROSS-PROCESS WINEVENT HOOK, AND NOTHING SELF-CORRECTS IT. mpv sizes its --wid child at creation from `GetClientRect(w32->parent)`, and afterwards only from `resize_child_win()`, which copies the PARENT's client rect onto the child. That has exactly two triggers, both on the parent's geometry: `parent_win_hook` — `SetWindowsHookExW(WH_CALLWNDPROC, parent_win_hook, NULL, tid)`, which mpv installs only when the parent lives inside its own process, never true here (mpv.exe is a separate process from Electron; main.js:861 hands over an HWND across a process boundary) — and `parent_evt_hook` — `SetWinEventHook(EVENT_OBJECT_LOCATIONCHANGE, ..., pid, tid, WINEVENT_OUTOFCONTEXT)`. So in THIS app precisely one asynchronous, coalescible, message-queue-delivered hook keeps the picture the size of the plane. A hook event missed, coalesced, or delivered while mpv's GUI thread is busy re-creating a swapchain leaves the child at the previous rect, anchored at the parent's origin (0,0) — a picture in the top-left region of the plane with the plane's own #08070c (main.js:768) filling the rest. And because `window_resize()` reads the CHILD's client rect back into vo->dwidth/dheight when parented, mpv then believes the stale rect and never corrects it. Any real parent geometry change re-fires the hook, which is precisely why dragging the window's scale is her workaround. Note what this changes: the transition correlation can still be genuine, but as a TIMING coincidence — the fullscreen (or maximise) geometry change racing mpv's VO reconfiguration — not as mpv choosing a size. THE DISCRIMINATING TEST, and it is cheaper than the six-step walk: measure the mpv child's rect against `GetClientRect` of the video plane's HWND across `video.setFullScreen(true)` ALONE, with no file change at all, repeated ~20 times, and again with a `loadfile` issued within ~100ms of the fullscreen call. If it goes stale with no transition anywhere near it, the episode transition is a red herring and every one of the three fixes is aimed at the wrong thing.
